## About me

I am a Computer Science Engineer and full-stack developer with experience across web, backend, mobile, desktop and enterprise software.

Over the years, I have worked on everything from hospital emergency-management systems and ERP/CRM implementations to multi-repository product platforms and AI-assisted internal tools. I am most comfortable in projects where the requirements are complex, integrations matter and the software needs to remain understandable long after the first release.

These days I work mainly with **Python, PHP, TypeScript and Flutter**. I also use AI-assisted engineering tools in my daily workflow, with the same rule I apply to any other tool: the result still needs thoughtful design, testing and human review.

## How I approach engineering

- **Keep the architecture proportional to the problem.** Not every feature needs a new abstraction or service.
- **Automate repetition, not judgment.** Tools should remove mechanical work and leave important decisions visible.
- **Prefer maintainable over clever.** Readable code and clear ownership usually age better than impressive shortcuts.
- **Treat AI output as a draft.** Review, test and accountability still belong to the engineer.
- **Understand the business process.** Good implementation starts before the first line of code.
